Most translation problems start in the original article. Before your help center goes multilingual, try these 4 habits:

1. Pick one name for each thing and stick with it.
2. Write short sentences with one idea each.
3. Skip idioms and jokes. "Hit it out of the park" rarely survives translation.
4. Keep text out of screenshots. Put it in the caption so it gets translated too.
