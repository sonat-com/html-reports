53.1% of the top 1 million home pages had images missing alt text (WebAIM Million, 2026).

Help articles lean on screenshots. So here's a simple habit: write alt text that says what the screenshot shows.

❌ "Screenshot 3"
✅ "Account settings, with the Save button highlighted at the bottom right"

Name the button, the setting, or the result. Screen reader users get the step too, and so does anyone whose image doesn't load.
