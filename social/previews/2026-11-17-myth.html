Myth: documentation is the docs team's job.

Reality: your docs team can't write down what they were never told. Most answers live with the people who field the questions: support, product, and ops.

Try this:
✍️ Let those experts write the first draft in a tool they already use
✅ Have the docs team review it before it's published
🔁 Keep one place where the final version lives

The experts bring what they know. The docs team keeps the quality high.
