Customers are asking AI for help. Just not the company chatbot.

A Gartner survey of 3,566 customers found they're about 3x more likely to use a third-party AI tool than a company's own chatbot for customer service. Use of those outside tools nearly doubled in a year. Company chatbot use hasn't measurably grown since 2022.

So more support happens outside your own channels. The part you still control is your public help center:
✅ Keep it public and easy to find
✅ Update articles when the product changes
✅ Answer one question per article, in plain words
