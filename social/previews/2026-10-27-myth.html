Myth: a long FAQ page is a help center. 🐋

A FAQ is good for quick, one-line answers. But many questions people bring to your help center need steps, and every new entry makes the right answer harder to find.

Try this instead:
✅ Spot the questions your team answers most often
✅ Turn each one into a step-by-step help article
✅ Keep the FAQ short, and link each question to its article

Your FAQ becomes the map. The articles do the helping.
