That question your team answers over and over? It deserves its own help article.

Here's a simple way to write one people actually find:
1. Start from the real question — pull it straight from your support inbox.
2. Answer it in the first two lines: outcome first, steps second.
3. Title it the way people search ("How to reset your password," not "Password Management").
4. Publish it where they already look, and add a "Was this helpful?" prompt so it keeps improving.
