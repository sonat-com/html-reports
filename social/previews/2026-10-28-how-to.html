A good "Getting started" article isn't a product tour. It gets one thing done. Here's how to write one:

1. Pick one first win: the first thing a new customer wants to do.
2. Get them there in 5 steps or fewer. Link to optional settings instead of explaining them.
3. Show what success looks like, so they know it worked.
4. End with links to the 2–3 tasks that come next.
