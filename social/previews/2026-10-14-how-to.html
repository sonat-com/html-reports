Removing an old help article is easy. Removing it without breaking anything takes 5 steps:

1. Find articles about features or screens you no longer have.
2. Decide whether to update it, merge it into a related article, or retire it.
3. Redirect its URL to the closest current article, so bookmarks and search results still land somewhere useful.
4. Update the links to it from your other articles.
5. Keep an archived copy in case you need it back.
