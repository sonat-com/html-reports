Myth: shorter docs are always better.

Short isn't the same as clear. A help article can be tight and still leave readers stuck because a step got cut.

Cut this:
• "Simply" and "just"
• "Please note that…"
• Intros that repeat the title

Keep this:
• Every step, in order
• Where to click and what happens next
• What to do if it doesn't work

Sonat's Readability guide scores each topic, so you know which articles to tighten first.
