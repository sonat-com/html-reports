Your customers are trying to help themselves. Most of them don't get all the way there. 📉

A Gartner survey of 5,728 customers found:
• 73% use self-service at some point
• Only 14% of issues get fully resolved there
• The most common reason it fails: people can't find content relevant to their issue (43% of cases)

Two quick fixes for your help center:
1️⃣ Title articles with the words customers actually search, not your internal feature names.
2️⃣ Add a simple "Was this helpful?" so readers can flag what's missing, then fill the gaps.
