Steps people actually finish: a 4-rule checklist 📋

1️⃣ One action per step.
"Open Settings and turn on sync" is two steps. Split it.

2️⃣ Start with a verb.
Click, open, type, select. The reader knows exactly what to do.

3️⃣ Say what they should see next.
"The Sync page opens." Now they know it worked.

4️⃣ Add a screenshot only where the screen is confusing.
Every extra image is one more thing to update when your product changes.
