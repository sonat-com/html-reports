Monthly Product Update

What's new in Sonat

September 2026  ·  New features, improvements & fixes

This update is all about AI that works alongside your team — an assistant that writes and edits with you inside the editor, insights into what your readers are really asking, and smarter, meaning-based search. Plus new ways to build pages, manage add-ons, and share private docs. Here's everything that's new.

✨  New Features

🤖

Meet the AI Assistant in your editor (Beta)

Open the new assistant panel right beside your content and ask it to draft a topic, restructure a page, or polish your writing. When it suggests changes to a topic, you see a clear before-and-after view and decide what to keep — nothing changes without your approval. It follows your writing guidelines and can even find and add fitting images for you.
📊

AI Insights: see what your readers are asking

A new AI Insights page shows the questions readers ask your AI, how often they got an answer, which topics were cited most, and — most usefully — the questions your docs don't cover yet. It's a ready-made to-do list for your next round of content. Available on Business and above; Free accounts can explore an example report.
💬

Conversational AI chat for your readers

Readers can now have a real back-and-forth with your documentation in a docked chat panel, with suggested follow-up questions and answers grounded in your own manuals. Available on Enterprise.
🧠

Semantic search that understands meaning

Search now understands what readers mean, not just the exact words they type — so “how do I reset my login” finds your “Changing your password” page. Turn it on from the new Search tab in Organization Settings. Available on Business and above.
🧩

A self-serve add-on catalog

Optional features now live in one Add-ons catalog where you can switch them on or off yourself, with clear descriptions, documentation links and Beta labels — no need to contact us to try something new.
✈️

Fleet documentation for aviation teams

Operators who maintain a QRH, MEL or operations manual across a fleet can now describe which options each aircraft has fitted and export a tailored PDF manual for every airframe — from a single source.
🖌

Custom HTML pages (Experimental)

Need a page that looks exactly the way you imagine? The new Custom HTML topic type lets you design fully custom pages — or have the AI Assistant build one for you — and publishes them safely alongside the rest of your docs, search-engine friendly on your own domain.

🚀  Improvements

🖨

A tab index on every PDF page

Printed and PDF manuals can now show a clickable tab index down the edge of every page, with the current section highlighted. Links between topics also jump straight to the right page inside the PDF, and diagrams print at the size you drew them.
🔗

Simpler private sharing

Private organization and document pages have been rebuilt so it's easy to see who has access. Share links now sit right alongside users and domains, show when they were created, and let you manage each link's reach from its own row.
🔎

Search that stays on track

You can now keep reader search within the current document, the sidebar filters down to matching topics as you type (in both the editor and your published docs), results appear in the reader's language, and a “Back to” link returns readers to where they were.
🌍

AI answers in your reader's language

AI answers now pull their sources in the language the question was asked in, and only respond when your documentation actually covers the question — so readers get answers they can trust.
⚙️

Choose your AI engine

Enterprise organizations can now choose which AI model powers their answers, including our premium GPT-5.6 Luna model.
💡

Share feedback in one click

A single Feedback form in the Help menu lets you report a problem or suggest an idea directly to our team — and the AI Assistant can connect you to live support chat whenever you ask.
⚡

Faster pages and a smoother editor

Images are now delivered in lighter formats so published pages load faster, and the editor stays responsive on large documents with many topics.
📈

Stronger SEO for published docs

Multilingual docs now tell search engines about every language version, and sitemaps include topics stored in folders — helping more readers find your content.

🐛  Bug Fixes
