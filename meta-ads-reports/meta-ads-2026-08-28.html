Recommendations

Framed as testable hypotheses, not directives. The account's official recommendations feed returned no items this week, so none are deferred to below. No pause/cut recommendation is made — there is no delivery to cut, and higher average cost alone is never a basis for cutting a segment.

  • 1. Diagnose the delivery gap first Priority
    Zero spend and zero impressions for a full week, right after a fully-delivering week, points to delivery having stopped rather than under-performing. Hypothesis: the campaign was intentionally ended/paused, or it hit a blocker (budget/schedule exhausted, billing threshold, or an asset/policy restriction). Check campaign status, schedule end date, budget, and account/asset health in Ads Manager. Expected impact: restoring delivery is the only way to resume any results — no spend means no impressions, clicks, or traffic.
  • 2. If traffic is still the goal, relaunch at the same efficient settings, then test scaling
    In its last active week the campaign showed a strong engagement profile for top-of-funnel traffic: CTR (all) 4.7%, CPC (Link Click) $0.17, and low Frequency (1.05), i.e. the audience was nowhere near fatigue. Hypothesis: the creative-and-audience combination is efficient at this small scale. Relaunch with the same setup, and test a modest budget increase to see whether efficiency holds as volume grows — treat any CPC/CPM rise as a signal to reassess, not a foregone conclusion. Expected impact: more Link Clicks while watching whether marginal cost stays acceptable.
  • 3. Add conversion tracking to measure business impact, not just clicks
    The campaign optimizes for Link Clicks and generated 99 landing page views last active week, but there is no downstream signal — no purchases, values, or Purchase ROAS in the data. Hypothesis: instrumenting standard events (or moving a future phase to a conversion objective) would let us judge whether this traffic produces real outcomes. Expected impact: turns an efficient-traffic story into a measurable business-value story and enables better budget decisions later.

Methodology note

This is a weekly executive digest built from aggregate account- and campaign-level totals for the reporting window and the immediately preceding equal-length window (for week-over-week deltas), pulled from the Meta Graph API (v21.0). It contains no day-by-day time series, no placement/audience/geo breakdowns, and no ad-set or ad-level data. It therefore reports what changed, not a deep root-cause diagnosis: it does not attribute movement to learning-phase status, auction overlap, relevance rankings, or marginal-CPA / Breakdown-Effect mechanics, because the aggregate data cannot support those claims. Money is shown in the account currency (USD). Metric names follow standardized display naming; audience is reported as “Accounts Center accounts”; “Clicks (all)” denotes total interactions while “Link Clicks” denotes offsite clicks.
