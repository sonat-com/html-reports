Treat this as a longer-term test, not an immediate switch — the current traffic setup is performing well on its own terms.

Methodology & scope

This is a weekly executive digest built from aggregate account- and campaign-level totals for the reporting window and the immediately preceding equal-length window (for week-over-week deltas). It does not include a day-by-day time series, placement / audience breakdowns, or ad-set / ad-level data, and it does not diagnose learning-phase status, auction overlap, marginal-CPA (Breakdown Effect), or relevance rankings — the underlying data can’t support those, and asserting them would be speculation. Accordingly, this report describes what changed week-over-week and frames every recommendation as a testable hypothesis, deferring to Meta’s official account recommendations where they apply.
