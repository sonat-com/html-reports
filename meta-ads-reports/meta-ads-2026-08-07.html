Recommendations

1. Test Advantage+ Audience on the ad setOfficial Meta rec

Meta's account recommendations estimate ~9.7% lower median cost per result from enabling Advantage+ Audience, which lets delivery expand beyond fixed audience settings when it is likely to improve performance (you can still enforce location and minimum-age limits).

Hypothesis & measure: enable it and compare CPC (Link Click) and Cost per Landing Page View against this week's baseline ($0.164 / $0.209). Given costs are already falling, this is a low-risk test to extend the efficiency trend rather than a change made on cost averages alone.

2. Add a video variant alongside the image adsOfficial Meta rec

Meta recommends a mix of image and video formats in the ad set — duplicate a current ad and swap in a video, keeping the original running so the system keeps optimizing.

Hypothesis & measure: a video variant may reach accounts the static creatives miss and lift CTR (all) beyond this week's 4.82%. Watch CTR and CPC (Link Click) on the new variant versus the incumbents; retain whichever the system favors.

3. Consider a controlled budget test while costs are favorableTestable hypothesis

CPM fell 10.9%, CPC (Link Click) fell 15.1%, and Frequency remains low at 1.08 — signs the account is buying efficient, non-saturated delivery. A modest, staged budget increase could capture more Link Clicks while unit costs are low.

Hypothesis & measure: raise budget in small increments and watch CPC (Link Click), CPM and Frequency for the first signs of rising cost or fatigue; scale back if unit costs climb. This is a test to improve overall results, not a directive — no segment is being paused or cut.

4. Instrument a downstream conversion eventMeasurement

With only one Complete Registration recorded this week, the campaign can be judged on traffic but not on a cost per business outcome. If registrations or calls are the real goal, define and fire a reliable conversion event so future digests can report Cost per Result and — where relevant — test a conversion objective.

Expected impact: better measurement, not a delivery change; it lets subsequent reports connect the strong traffic efficiency shown here to real business results.

Methodology note. This is a weekly executive digest built from aggregate account- and campaign-level totals for Aug 1–7, 2026 versus the preceding equal-length window (Jul 25–31, 2026), pulled from the Meta Graph API (v21.0). It intentionally reports what changed. It does not attempt deep root-cause diagnosis: the data contains no daily time series, no placement/audience/geographic breakdowns, and no ad-set- or ad-level detail, so learning-phase status, marginal-CPA / Breakdown-Effect reasoning, auction-overlap and relevance-ranking analysis are out of scope for this report. Every figure is taken directly from the API response; metrics absent from the data (e.g. Purchases and Purchase ROAS, which this Link Clicks campaign does not track) are reported as absent rather than estimated. Audience figures are Accounts Center accounts. "Clicks (all)" counts all interactions; "Link Clicks" counts offsite link clicks — they are distinct metrics.
