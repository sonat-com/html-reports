Test: a modest, incremental budget increase while watching Frequency, CPM, and CTR (all). Expected impact: more Link Clicks at similar efficiency, so long as Frequency and CPM stay stable.

Hypothesis: adding standard conversion events would let future weeks judge true business outcomes, not just clicks. Test: confirm/implement conversion tracking before considering an objective change. Expected impact: optimization and reporting aligned to real outcomes, improving decisions about where overall budget should go.
