Methodology

This is a weekly executive digest built from aggregate account- and campaign-level totals for the reporting window and the immediately preceding window of equal length (pulled from the Meta Graph API, v21.0). It reports what changed week over week.

It intentionally does not attempt deep root-cause diagnosis: the data contains no daily time series, no placement/audience/device breakdowns, and no ad-set or ad-level detail, so mechanisms such as marginal-CPA shifts, the Breakdown Effect, learning-phase status, or auction overlap cannot be evidenced and are not asserted. Metric names follow standardized Meta reporting conventions; audience size is reported as Accounts Center accounts; "Clicks (all)" (total interactions) and "Link Clicks" (offsite clicks) are kept distinct. All figures are drawn directly from the fetched data — none are estimated.
